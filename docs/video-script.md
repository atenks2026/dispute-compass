# Demo Video Script — Dispute Compass (target: 2:40 of 3:00 max)

> Recording notes: 1440×900 browser viewport, Demo Mode (no credentials), narration in a calm, confident tone. Every scene shows the product working — no slides except the closing card. Demo dates are relative to server start; the countdown badge floors partial days, so case PP-D-27401 shows **4 days left** throughout a run (20-day window minus 15 days elapsed). Narration matches the on-screen number.

---

## Scene 0 — Hook (0:00–0:12) · screen: case PP-D-27401 cockpit

**VO:** "A PayPal dispute just landed. The buyer wants two hundred forty-eight dollars. The inquiry auto-closes in four days — and if it does, that's it. Closed disputes can never be reopened. Most people don't lose because they're wrong. They lose because they missed a window, or sent the wrong evidence."

## Scene 1 — The cockpit (0:12–0:35) · screen: portfolio + KPI strip

**VO:** "This is Dispute Compass, an AI case coach for PayPal disputes, built on the PayPal Disputes API. The portfolio — powered by AG Grid — sorts every case by the deadline that's closest to firing. Up top: active cases, deadlines inside two days, open appeal windows, and the money actually in play."

## Scene 2 — Deadline Engine (0:35–1:00) · screen: PP-D-27401 deadline card

**VO:** "The Deadline Engine is pure rules — never AI guesses. It knows the inquiry auto-closes twenty days after opening, that sellers get about ten days to respond to a claim, and that appeals live in a ten-day window after a decision. For each window it shows why the deadline exists, what happens if you miss it, and what to do now."

## Scene 3 — Strength Meter + Settlement Advisor (1:00–1:30) · screen: PP-D-27401 (Evidence & Strength tab)

**VO:** "The Strength Meter scores the evidence the way a reviewer reads it: tracking, proof of delivery, address match. Watch the score climb as items are verified. The Settlement Advisor turns that score into money: accept for the certain cost, offer around sixty percent here, or fight with a twenty-four percent win chance. No bravado — just math."

## Scene 4 — AI Case Coach (1:30–1:55) · screen: PP-D-27401 coach tab (brief generated with POD verified; Settlement Advisor above updates to ~45% offer)

**VO:** "For the language side, the AI Case Coach reads the whole case file and briefs you: what's verifiably strong, what a reviewer will question, the best opening move, and the next actions in order. It's grounded in the case facts and a curated policy library — it cites policy IDs, it never invents them."

## Scene 5 — Letter Studio + Integrity Guard (1:55–2:25) · screen: PP-D-27404 letter flow

**VO:** "The Letter Studio drafts the appeal. Notice it must argue *new* evidence — here, the carrier confirmed the parcel was lost after the decision. Before anything can be sent, the Integrity Guard flags absolute claims, and three attestations are mandatory. AI maximizes complete truth — not engineered victories." *(click Submit appeal → "Sent to PayPal Sandbox" / "Recorded in Demo Mode")*

## Scene 6 — Live sandbox + close (2:25–2:40) · screen: header badges + closing card

**VO:** "Out of the box it runs in Demo Mode — no credentials needed. Plug in PayPal Sandbox keys and it reads your real cases; add an LLM key and the coach goes fully generative. Dispute Compass: know where you stand, settle smart, escalate right."

**Closing card:** logo + tagline + repo URL.
